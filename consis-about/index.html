Our Vision

A greener skyline — where every wall, façade and
structure can become a thriving piece of nature.

As cities grow taller and denser, Consis Engineering exists to put nature back where it belongs — in the heart of the built environment. We design, build and maintain living green walls that cool buildings, clean the air, delight people and prove that high-density living and lush greenery can grow side by side.

Our Story

Pioneering vertical greenery in Singapore since 2006.

In 2006, the Consis Vertical Greenery System was unveiled to the public at the Singapore Garden Festival — and it has been our flagship product ever since. Over the years, Consis VGS has earned a loyal following among architects, landscape architects and discerning clients for its winning combination of easy installation, immediate results, high durability, low maintenance and full customisation.

The proven system is fully integrated with automatic irrigation, drainage and fertilisation functions, providing a holistic ecology in which plants are ideally placed to grow and thrive. Since its debut showcase, Consis VGS has been brought to life across numerous projects in Singapore and overseas.

The System Behind the Green

A complete, living ecology — engineered

Consis VGS is more than a planter. It is a fully integrated system that gives every single plant exactly what it needs to thrive.

Individual Pot System

Each plant grows naturally and vertically in its own pot, giving foliage a realistic, natural look — and letting any plant be replaced easily.

Irrigation · Fertigation · Drainage

A fully integrated system gives every plant its own automatic supply of water and fertiliser, with built-in drainage for a balanced, holistic ecology.

Lightweight Modular Frame

At just 3 kg/m² (system only) and around 50 kg/m² when saturated and mature, the frame is light enough for non-structural walls and safe for structures of all types.

2D & 3D Designs

Choose attractive two-dimensional (flat) or sculptural three-dimensional configurations to match the architecture and creative intent.
